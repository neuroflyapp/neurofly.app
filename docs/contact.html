Contact & suggestions

Questions about the model, collaboration, teaching use or early access — or an idea for making NeuroFly better. Scientific corrections are especially welcome.

Your message is forwarded to our mailbox by Airform, an external form service, which then shows its own confirmation page. How we handle your details: privacy notice. You can also write to contact@neurofly.app directly.

For a correction, please say where (page, figure, workspace) and, if you can, give a reference.

Optional — only if you would like a reply.

Sent through the Airform form service to our mailbox; see the privacy notice. By sending a suggestion you agree to the terms for submissions.

What happens next

Messages are read by a person. We answer questions and collaboration requests where we can; suggestions are reviewed and may be taken up in a later release.

Scientific corrections are checked against the data and literature, and confirmed errors are corrected on the Evidence page.

You can also write to us directly: contact@neurofly.app.

Please note

NeuroFly is a research and teaching tool. It is not a medical device and gives no advice on health, medicines or the treatment of animals.
