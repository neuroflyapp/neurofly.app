01 / Abstract

What the connectome tells us.
And what it does not.

Synapse-resolution wiring constrains which cells can interact. It does not specify their physiology, behavior, drug response or subjective experience. NeuroCause makes that distinction inspectable rather than hiding it inside a simulation.

The current running model joins a selected female FAFB/FlyWire brain graph to a selected male MaleCNS nerve-cord graph through an explicitly modelled population-rate interface. Its body, sensory encoders and neural dynamics are model components. The paper documents the sources and choices, the checks that pass, the phenomena that do not reproduce and the empirical gates for stronger claims.

02 / Architecture

One explicit bridge.
No invented cross-animal synapses.

The active closed loop uses two biological specimens. Published neuron identities and contact counts are kept distinct from the engineered bridge and virtual body. Selecting another anatomy explorer does not silently substitute it into the running circuit.

Female BANC and male MaleCNS each contain brain and cord from one animal, but the active runtime does not yet use a single-specimen brain-to-body model. MANC is a different male individual. The optic-lobe v1.1 explorer is an older overlapping release of the same MaleCNS male; its connections are not added to the newer graph.

Three evidential layers.
Do not collapse them.
03 / Evidence

Anatomy, simulation, experience.

The paper distinguishes published reconstructions from computed states and from the unresolved question of experience. A numerical output is not automatically a biological measurement.

A / Source-derived

Reconstructed anatomy

Versioned cell identities, selected directed neuron pairs and anatomical contact counts.

Does not determine conductance or receptor effects.
B / Model-derived

Simulated dynamics

Seeded membrane states, spikes, behavior and responses to interventions.

Not yet calibrated to withheld recordings or another animal.
C / Unresolved

Subjective experience

No direct observable in the current software establishes feeling or pain.

Digital sentience remains a research question, not a result.

Internal software tests passed for specimen identity, graph validity, reproducibility, sensory routing and exports. The documented model does not reproduce habituation, associative learning or thermal preference in the cited protocols. These failures are reported rather than hidden. See the benchmark evidence and the full paper for methods and references.

04 / Claim boundaries

A research prototype.
Not a replacement animal.

Whole animal

The running brain and cord come from different animals. The virtual eye, muscles and body were not measured from either reconstructed individual.

Pharmacology

Generic gain or blockade controls are circuit interventions. Predicting a named compound requires targets, concentration-response curves, uptake, metabolism and blinded validation.

Sentience

NeuroCause does not report measured feelings, pain or a sentience score. Whether a digital system could have subjective experience is an open research and ethics question.

05 / Prospective tests

What evidence must come next?

  1. 01
    One specimen, one native graph.

    Audit brain-to-cord coverage, missing edges and version differences before a same-animal runtime is claimed.

  2. 02
    Calibrate physiology.

    Fit identifiable sensory, cellular and motor parameters against independent recordings, with uncertainty.

  3. 03
    Predict held-out data.

    Freeze code and parameters, preregister endpoints and compare with simpler baselines in blinded neural and behavioral experiments.

  4. 04
    Assess sentience separately.

    Define competing theories and discriminating tests with independent ethical review; prediction alone cannot establish experience.
